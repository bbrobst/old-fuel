Project Overview

The Research Data Portal is a web-based platform designed for managing, submitting, updating, and deleting research datasets. It provides an intuitive interface for researchers, analysts, and administrators to interact with curated data collections. The portal supports open access principles and streamlines data management workflows for academic and professional use.
