Introduction

Old Fuel uses the classic Auto MPG dataset from the UCI Machine Learning Repository. The data includes 398 cars from 1970 to 1982, with information on each vehicle's city-cycle fuel consumption, engine displacement, horsepower, weight, acceleration, cylinders, model year, and origin (USA, Europe, or Japan). Eight instances were removed from the original dataset, which could introduce minor bias, but all remaining records are complete with no missing values. This data powers our fuel economy lookups and estimates for vintage car enthusiasts and shoppers.
